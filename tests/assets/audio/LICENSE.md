## Modifications

The audio test file was derived from Lee Galloway's original recording with the following modifications:

- Converted from OGG Vorbis to WAV.
- Resampled to 48 kHz stereo, 16-bit PCM.
- Trimmed to 50 seconds.
- Applied a 2-second fade-out at the end.

This modified audio recording is distributed under **CC BY-SA 3.0**.
